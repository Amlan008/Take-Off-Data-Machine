Experimental automated analyses, not official advisories. H/L: pressure extrema; dashed red/blue: trough/ridge; blue triangles: cold-front candidates; red semicircles: warm-front candidates; alternating opposite-side symbols: stationary candidates; purple paired symbols: occlusion candidates; dashed orange: unclassified boundaries. Purple arrows: jet candidates ≥60 kt. Air-mass patches show supported thermal/moisture regions, not source attribution or tracked parcels; optional advection patches show diagnosed warming/cooling. Front types require agreement along the boundary and temporal, thermal and wind evidence; ambiguous evidence stays unclassified. Click a feature for evidence. Cyclonic lows show a Gray-style environmental checklist, not a genesis probability. Ocean heat and organized convection are unassessed; warm-core evidence alone never establishes tropical-cyclone status.

Smooth contours combine land, airport and marine observations. Reported MSLP is preferred; where missing, airport QNH is converted to estimated station pressure using the standard atmosphere, then reduced to sea level using temperature and elevation. These estimates are labelled and given lower weight, especially over high terrain. Temperature charts include a °C colour scale. Interpolation extends across oceans within 2000 km for pressure/upper-air fields and 1500 km for surface temperature, requiring at least three reports; sparse-area values are less certain and unsupported areas remain blank. No model background is used. Wind barbs retain platform measurement differences. Upper-air charts show nominal sounding times; click a station for values and launch time.

Sources: NOAA MADIS METAR and maritime · DWD international SYNOP · NOAA MADIS radiosonde (primary) · University of Wyoming radiosondes (gap fill). Each three-hourly run rechecks the latest and preceding 00/12 UTC sounding cycle for late reports. Recent charts are retained for 48 hours.
