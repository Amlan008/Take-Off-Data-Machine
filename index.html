    METHOD
    What is being corrected?
    
        
    Each available Open-Meteo model is compared with the closest AWC METAR observation. Recent observations receive more influence, outlying errors are constrained, and each continuous parameter is weighted by each model’s recent local skill. Wind is bias-corrected as east–west and north–south vector components before being converted back to aviation wind direction and speed; light-wind directions receive less influence because they are inherently variable. Rain/precipitation, thunderstorm, and fog/low-visibility signals are separately verified against the previous 72 hours of raw METAR and blended as skill-weighted event likelihoods. METAR variable winds are filled from the last actual direction or circularly interpolated between surrounding actual reports. Precipitation amount remains uncorrected because METAR does not supply a comparable amount.
