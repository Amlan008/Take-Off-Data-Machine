How this page will assess an advisory

Official advisory detail

For each active VAA, Weather Machine will present the agency-issued advisory text and graphic, volcano name and location, advisory issue time, observed ash cloud, flight-level band, movement, and agency forecast positions.

Airport proximity screen

Forecast ash-cloud coordinates will be screened against your local airport inventory. Airport names will be colour-coded by geographic proximity only.

  • Red — close to a current or forecast ash area
  • Amber — within the wider screening area
  • Green — outside the proximity screen

Operational limitation

This is a preliminary geographic screen, not an ash-concentration, route-clearance, engine-limit, dispatch or airspace decision. Always use the latest VAA, volcanic-ash SIGMET, NOTAM, air traffic control information and operator procedures.

Live-feed connection

This page uses the public Australian Bureau of Meteorology all-VAAC feed. It republishes official VAA text received from the nine VAACs, allowing independent use without an account. WIFS remains an optional future source for direct structured data and graphics.

Volcanic Ash Advisory Centres

The nine ICAO-designated VAACs are responsible for monitoring and forecasting ash dispersion within their areas of responsibility. Weather Machine will identify the responsible VAAC on every advisory and link through to the originating agency wherever it provides a public product page.
