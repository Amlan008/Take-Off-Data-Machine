Developer's Note

The Take-off Data Machine is a personal project created by meteorologist Amlan Chatterjee and his wife, Swarnali Chakraborty, a professional Data Engineer.

As the application gradually reaches more users, we remain committed to improving it through better technology and community feedback. However, no tool can replace the knowledge, experience and judgement of pilots, dispatchers and meteorologists.

This application uses publicly available weather and aviation data and is intended only as supplementary guidance. It must not replace official products, operational procedures or professional judgement. The developers accept no liability for any consequences arising from its use.
