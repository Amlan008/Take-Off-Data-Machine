Developer’s note & limitations

Weather machine is a personal project by meteorologist Amlan Chatterjee and his wife, data engineer Swarnali Chakraborty. A shared effort to bring better context to weather and aviation.

As the application reaches more users, we remain committed to improving it through better technology and community feedback. No tool can replace the knowledge, experience and judgement of pilots, dispatchers and meteorologists.

This application uses publicly available weather and aviation data as supplementary guidance. Weather Journal shows model-derived local conditions and forecasts; its images are illustrative. It must not replace official products, operational procedures or professional judgement. The developers accept no liability for consequences arising from its use.
