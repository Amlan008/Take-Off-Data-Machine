SCREENING REFERENCE

Weather-category rules

Marginal

  • -RA, DZ, -SN, BLDU or SHRA with gusts 15–19 kt and visibility ≤2000 m.
  • Any condition: below landing_minima_m + 200 m.
  • Any condition with no listed minima: visibility 1600–1800 m.

Bad

  • RA, -TSRA, TSRA, DSTS, DS, TS, SN, -RA, +SHRA or -SN.
  • Any condition with no listed minima: visibility <1600 m / at or below 1 SM; with minima: at/below landing_minima_m.
  • Any condition: FEW, SCT, BKN or OVC at or below FL003.

Severe

  • +RA, +TSRA, +TS, +SN, TSGR, TSGS, TSRAGR or TSRAGS.
  • SA, SS, BLSA, DRSA, DS or DSTS with gusts ≥25 kt; +DSTS; DRSN/BLSN with gusts ≥25 kt; or any gust >29 kt.
  • Any condition with no listed minima: visibility <1001 m / at or below 0.5 SM; with minima: at/below landing_minima_m − 100 m.
