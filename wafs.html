LAYER GUIDE

Official SIGMETs

Current international and domestic aviation warnings shown as issuing-agency polygons. Click a polygon for its bulletin information.

    Operational use. SIGMETs are official warnings. The GFS turbulence, icing and convection layers are model-derived diagnostics, not official aviation forecasts or WAFS products. They are intended for broad situational awareness and must be checked against current SIGMETs, PIREPs/AIREPs, volcanic-ash products, NOTAMs and company procedures.

    DATA STATUS

    What is displayed

    Official SIGMETs

    Worldwide current aviation-warning polygons obtained during the scheduled build and retained as same-origin GeoJSON.

    GFS turbulence

    Potential based on wind speed, horizontal wind gradient and vertical wind shear. It does not identify a guaranteed turbulence type.

    GFS icing

    Potential based on flight-level temperature and relative humidity. It is not an official airframe-icing forecast.

    GFS wind / temperature

    Sampled upper-air wind and temperature values at the selected flight level.

    GFS convection

    Potential based on CAPE and total cloud cover. Consult official convective products for operations.

    Update cycle

    Layers are generated from the latest available 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC GFS cycle by GitHub Actions.
