DATA RESOURCES

Official agency sources

Weather machine displays tropical-cyclone information supplied by the agencies below. NHC and CPHC provide the official forecast products for the Eastern and Central Pacific; JTWC provides the Western Pacific warning, track graphic and forecast discussion; IMD is retained for Indian Ocean products when its public feed is available. NRL supports the current-system catalogue and is not used to create an independent forecast.
